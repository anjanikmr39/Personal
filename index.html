10 Pranayam Benefits for Your Recovery
5 Kapalbhati • 5 Anulom Vilom

Kapalbhati (5 Benefits)

  • Clears & Energizes Airways: Vigorous exhalations help clear stale air as breathing capacity improves.
  • Counters Numbness & Low Energy: Rhythmic brisk breathing provides a natural wake-up boost.
  • Sharpens Mental Alertness: Helps cut through brain fog for clearer daytime focus.
  • Supports Digestion & Core Rhythm: Rhythmic abdominal contractions support healthy digestion and gut flow.
  • Builds Strong Morning Discipline: A focused breath routine anchors a healthy new daily pattern.

Anulom Vilom (5 Benefits)

  • Calms Evening Restlessness: Slow, steady breathing helps ease agitation when evening urges hit.
  • Supports a Calm Nervous System: Smooth inhales and exhales encourage relaxation without sugar spikes.
  • Improves Breath Pacing: Controlled alternate-nostril breathing builds calm pacing when urges rise.
  • Promotes Emotional Steadiness: Helps steady mood swings and mild irritability during early recovery.
  • Creates a Grounding Reset: Offers a quiet pause before stepping out or winding down for the night.

Practice gently on an empty stomach, ease off if dizzy, and consult a doctor first if you have respiratory, heart, or blood pressure concerns.
